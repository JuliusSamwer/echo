World-Model Planning Ladder · rung 0 → 3

Zeus

A reward-free agent that reaches goals by imagining rollouts in a learned model and steering toward the goal in latent space — built one falsifiable rung at a time.

Thesis. A flat world model + sampling planner is enough for short horizons, but breaks predictably as the path grows. The remedy — temporal abstraction — should be earned by planning, not fixed by hand or found by compression. Each rung proves the floor, finds the break, adds hierarchy, then asks whether the abstraction can emerge at the maze's decision points.

ENV

The worlds

Two mazes. The agent sees only its position — never the walls — so a single flat model is necessarily one-maze-specific. That limit is the whole reason to graduate the encoder later.

Grid point-maze · rungs 0–2

start goal (ε=0.2) optimal path (BFS = L) planned trajectory

Perfect maze (unique path). Action ±0.4 per axis, < 1 cell → each step crosses at most one wall. Reward is always 0; success = inside the goal radius.

Bottleneck maze · rung 3, certified

doorway (bottleneck) GT — threads doors ADV — stalls at wall

3×3 rooms joined by off-center doorways. A subgoal at a doorway succeeds where one placed elsewhere on the same route fails — placement is causally load-bearing. L = doorways on the route.

MODELS

The learned pieces

Small, deliberate, mostly frozen. Everything speaks latents; the planner never touches raw obs.

E / encoder

Affine, invertible normalizer. Latent is the state, rescaled to O(1). The graduation seam — swap for a pixel encoder later.

in  obs (·,2)
out z = (obs−μ)/σ

P / forward model

Residual MLP. A zero-init linear skip learns free-space motion; the MLP only fits the sparse wall corrections. 3×256, GELU.

in  z, a
out Δz → z′ = z + Δz
err ≈ 0.09–0.11 cell/step

G / coarse dynamics

One-shot k-step jump in latent space over a macro-action m (a VAE-encoded action chunk). No intra-macro compounding.

in  z_b, m
out Δz to next boundary

B / boundary gate

Reads [z, z−z₋₁] → g∈[0,1]: "is this a chunk boundary?" Gumbel-sigmoid, self-sharpens. The emergent abstraction.

in  z_t, z_{t−1}
out g_t ∈ [0,1]
Training P: offline random-walk transitions, latent-space loss = 1-step teacher-forced MSE + K=8-step autoregressive rollout MSE. Only P is learned; E is fixed. At rung 3, E and P are frozen — B, G and the action-VAE learn on top.
PLAN

How the planner thinks

Sampling MPC. No gradients through actions — it guesses action sequences, scores them by imagined rollout, and refines toward the goal.

sampled rollouts (N) elite set refined mean plan goal

A cartoon of one CEM step: fan out N action sequences from the start, keep the ones whose imagined rollout gets closest to the goal, refit, repeat.

CEM / MPPI — one planning call

1 · sample — draw N action seqs (N,H,2) ~ 𝒩(mean, std), clip to ±a_max.
2 · imagine — roll P forward: (N,H,D) latent trajectories.
3 · score — goal energy = min over horizon ‖z_goal − rollout‖. "Did it ever reach?" — robust to overshoot & wall detours.
4 · refine — CEM keeps top elites & refits (std floored to keep exploring); MPPI takes a softmax-weighted mean.
Receding horizon: execute the first k actions, warm-start the next plan, replan. Stalled? cold-start + inject exploration to break the wall-hugging deadlock. Planner cost = N·H per plan.

RUNG 3

Where abstraction must come from

Same boundary net, three different pressures. The contrast is the experiment.

A trajectory's boundary gates. Spikes = chunk breaks. The claim under test: with the right pressure, the spikes land on junctions / doorways — measured by junction lift = P(junction | boundary) ÷ P(junction | any step). Lift = 1 → random. Lift > 1 → structure found.

SSL-only

control

Sufficiency + closure + rate. Pure compression. Prediction: lift ≈ 1.0 — compression alone cannot find decision points.

Planning-pressure

test

B chases the reachability residual — which spikes exactly where a chunk would hit a wall or round a turn. Reward the longest reachable chunk. Hypothesis: lift > 1.

REINFORCE probe

de-risk

One binary question on the real planner: does the residual push boundaries toward junctions at all? Gate: lift > 1.15.

GATE

Certifying the env before training

Junction lift only means something if placement actually helps. The probe proves it — hand-placed subgoals, no learning — for the cost of zero gradient steps.

A narrow placement-critical band. Too little budget (H=8) → even GT fails. Too much (H≥11) → the low-level threads doorways unaided and the GT−ADV gap collapses. H=10 is the sweet spot.

GT ADVERSARIAL gap (certifies)
Why off-center doorways? With centered doors, a room-center subgoal sits collinear with the gap — so aiming at the room threads the door for free and placement never bites. Jitter breaks that collinearity. This is the one design change that turned a failing env into a certified one.
